<a id="c-001--q-021"></a>
## C-001 — Q-021

Comparative evolutionary forecasting of visual receptors, anatomy and behaviour; estimate conditional future adaptations rather than a unique next species.

Historical sources: HO-001, HO-003.
Related existing IDs: Q-013.

Proposed test / boundary: Phylogenetically held-out species, calibrated uncertainty, simple baselines; distinguish genotype, spectral response and behaviour. Model of evolution, not an evolving model.

<a id="c-002--q-022"></a>
## C-002 — Q-022

Can sensing range, modality, sensitivity and actuator morphology coevolve with controllers?

Historical sources: HO-003, HO-007, HO-011, HO-013, HO-015.
Related existing IDs: H-015, Q-004.

Proposed test / boundary: Heritable sensor variation, matched costs, shuffled/misleading cue, sensor-controller ablations.

<a id="c-003--q-023"></a>
## C-003 — Q-023

When does a selective, incomplete representation outperform a more accurate or comprehensive one?

Historical sources: HO-001, HO-002, HO-011, HO-015, HO-016.
Related existing IDs: See central Q-001.

Proposed test / boundary: Independently vary accuracy, bandwidth, information relevance and cost; measure fitness separately from reconstruction error.

<a id="c-004--q-024"></a>
## C-004 — Q-024

Can agents infer hidden environmental variables and distinguish stable causal rules from temporary noise or coincidence?

Historical sources: HO-002, HO-006, HO-017.
Related existing IDs: Q-011.

Proposed test / boundary: Intervention versus observation; matched latent models; held-out shifts and genuine/noise signals; report underdetermination.

<a id="c-005--q-025"></a>
## C-005 — Q-025

Do event-based time, internal oscillators, temporal memory or prediction offer advantages under recurring or irregular conditions?

Historical sources: HO-004, HO-010, HO-012, HO-014, HO-015, HO-016.
Related existing IDs: See central Q-001.

Proposed test / boundary: Match temporal information access; vary phase, period, jitter and predictability; no subjective-time claim.

<a id="c-006--q-004"></a>
## C-006 — Q-004

How should agents allocate limited sensory sampling and computational bandwidth over time?

Historical sources: HO-002, HO-004, HO-011, HO-012.
Related existing IDs: Q-004.

Proposed test / boundary: Equal compute; compare continuous, selective and adaptive allocation; costs, latency and performance; no universal conscious bits/s.

<a id="c-007--q-026"></a>
## C-007 — Q-026

Does adaptive weighting of expectations and observations improve control following shifts or under noisy cues?

Historical sources: HO-001, HO-011, HO-012.
Related existing IDs: F-007, F-012.

Proposed test / boundary: Compare fixed weights, learned weights, simple filters and uncertainty-sensitive inference; separate prediction and fitness.

## C-008 — Q-011/Q-016

What are the comparative benefits of reactive control, RL, predictive control and formal active inference?

Historical sources: HO-004, HO-008, HO-011, HO-012.
Related existing IDs: Q-011, Q-016.

Proposed test / boundary: Matched sensory data, resource and compute budgets; specify actual generative/inference models; distinguish predictions from action outcomes.

<a id="c-009--q-027"></a>
## C-009 — Q-027

Does an explicit persistent internal state enable useful memory without a neural network?

Historical sources: HO-007, HO-008, HO-009.
Related existing IDs: H-008.

Proposed test / boundary: Compare reactive, leaky/persistent state and explicit episodic memory; delayed perturbation, reset and cost tests.

## C-010 — H-008/M-007

Can developmental schedules and heritable learning rates balance adaptation, forgetting and retention?

Historical sources: HO-005, HO-007, HO-012.
Related existing IDs: H-008, M-007.

Proposed test / boundary: Constant vs scheduled vs adaptive plasticity; same lifetime budget; drift and hereditary controls; reversal and novel tasks.

<a id="c-011--q-016"></a>
## C-011 — Q-016

Does embodied interaction support better transfer than passive observations or symbolic instruction alone?

Historical sources: HO-002, HO-005, HO-013.
Related existing IDs: H-016.

Proposed test / boundary: Factor observation/action/feedback; match information and training resources; distinguish actuator constraints from learning competence.

<a id="c-012--q-028"></a>
## C-012 — Q-028

Can selection favour memory consolidation or offline simulation despite missed resource acquisition opportunities?

Historical sources: HO-012, HO-013.
Related existing IDs: Q-004.

Proposed test / boundary: Continuous, inactive, offline replay and simulated planning controls with equal total budgets; no sleep or dreaming equivalence.

## C-013 — Q-006/H-009

Which ancestral successes, failures and unfinished objectives are worth transmitting?

Historical sources: HO-005, HO-011, HO-016.
Related existing IDs: H-009.

Proposed test / boundary: Match information budgets; success-only/failure-only/both/blank, goal transfer with changed feasibility; log deaths without magically granting agent access to archive.

<a id="c-014--q-029"></a>
## C-014 — Q-029

Can inherited records be compressed, selectively retrieved or reinterpreted without sacrificing novel-environment adaptation?

Historical sources: HO-002, HO-004, HO-013, HO-016.
Related existing IDs: H-009.

Proposed test / boundary: Raw versus summary versus rules and hierarchical versus flat memory, matched capacity and costs; stable/reversal/novel contexts.

<a id="c-015--q-030"></a>
## C-015 — Q-030

What transmission fidelity, repair, redundancy and mutation rates support cumulative adaptation under information damage?

Historical sources: HO-001, HO-005, HO-013, HO-016.
Related existing IDs: H-009.

Proposed test / boundary: Independently perturb genotype, state, sensor and archive; measure error, cost, lineage diversity and recovery; not biological radiation or cancer equivalence.

## C-016 — Q-006/H-014/H-017

Can knowledge persist beyond generations and catastrophes without conflating researcher archives, genetic transfer, acquired state, culture and environmental inheritance?

Historical sources: HO-000, HO-005, HO-009, HO-010, HO-015, HO-016, HO-017.
Related existing IDs: Q-006.

Proposed test / boundary: Separate channels; complete-reset/partial-retention conditions, record extinction and misinformation; archives inaccessible unless protocol specifies transmission.

<a id="c-017--q-031"></a>
## C-017 — Q-031

How do multiple competing physiological requirements and harmful resource excess create priorities?

Historical sources: HO-009, HO-010, HO-012, HO-015.
Related existing IDs: Q-001, Q-004.

Proposed test / boundary: Multiple viability variables, matched resource gradients; compare fixed rules and learned prioritisation; no programmed intelligent reward.

<a id="c-018--q-032"></a>
## C-018 — Q-032

Can regulation of regulation, metabolic flexibility or dynamic internal processing become selectively beneficial?

Historical sources: HO-010, HO-012.
Related existing IDs: Q-011.

Proposed test / boundary: Automatic vs adaptive vs higher-order controllers, perturb mechanisms, match costs; does not establish free will.

<a id="c-019--q-033"></a>
## C-019 — Q-033

When does persistence, goal revision or counterfactual planning outperform immediate response?

Historical sources: HO-004, HO-011, HO-012.
Related existing IDs: H-011.

Proposed test / boundary: Block resources, change feasibility; compare rigid persistence, exploration, reactive and planning mechanisms; cost/time limits; no quantum timeline claim.

<a id="c-020--q-034"></a>
## C-020 — Q-034

Can metacognitive confidence estimates and recursive self-evaluation improve adaptation?

Historical sources: HO-004, HO-010, HO-012.
Related existing IDs: Q-011, Q-012.

Proposed test / boundary: Calibrated uncertainty, causal interventions, recursive depth and cost sweeps; compare confidence to actual reliability.

<a id="c-021--q-035"></a>
## C-021 — Q-035

Can agents distinguish self-caused versus externally caused changes and maintain useful self-models?

Historical sources: HO-004, HO-010, HO-011, HO-012.
Related existing IDs: Q-012.

Proposed test / boundary: Matched perturbations, state-report intervention, no-self-model controls; distinguish functional representation from identity and subjective experience.

<a id="c-022--q-036"></a>
## C-022 — Q-036

Do global access or selective broadcasting improve coordination over local, centralized or distributed alternatives?

Historical sources: HO-001, HO-004, HO-008, HO-011, HO-012, HO-015.
Related existing IDs: See central Q-001.

Proposed test / boundary: Factor predictive vs nonpredictive and workspace vs nonworkspace; matched budgets, ablation and novel contexts; distinguish GWT/GNWT and consciousness.

<a id="c-023--q-037"></a>
## C-023 — Q-037

Can initially incidental traces become useful cues and later controllable signals through exaptation?

Historical sources: HO-007, HO-008, HO-013.
Related existing IDs: H-013, H-017.

Proposed test / boundary: Controls for common causes, shared inherited rules, passive traces, controllable signals and misleading cues; intervention to establish causal signalling benefit.

<a id="c-024--q-038"></a>
## C-024 — Q-038

Can culturally transmitted symbols acquire functional meaning and cumulative utility?

Historical sources: HO-007, HO-010, HO-013, HO-016.
Related existing IDs: H-014.

Proposed test / boundary: Compare copying, imitation and signal conventions; held-out context and compositional tests; no inference of subjective understanding.

## C-025 — Q-014/H-013/Q-007

When do cooperation, truthful/deceptive signalling, restraint and exploitation affect lineage or ecosystem persistence?

Historical sources: HO-005, HO-008, HO-010.
Related existing IDs: H-013, Q-007.

Proposed test / boundary: Repeated vs one-off interaction, relatedness and scarcity; signal interventions; report conflict and extinction without moral reward.

<a id="c-026--q-039"></a>
## C-026 — Q-039

Can independently viable components form higher-order evolutionary individuals?

Historical sources: HO-004, HO-006, HO-008, HO-009, HO-014.
Related existing IDs: Q-007, Q-014.

Proposed test / boundary: Require boundaries, dependence, reproduction/heredity and group-level selection evidence; cooperation alone insufficient.

<a id="c-027--q-040"></a>
## C-027 — Q-040

Can resource recycling, niche construction and emergent ecosystem recovery arise, or does increased capability cause collapse?

Historical sources: HO-005, HO-009, HO-017.
Related existing IDs: H-017, Q-010.

Proposed test / boundary: Recyclable matter with external energy, no ecosystem-repair reward; disturbances, recovery vs altered equilibria and collapse; measure individual and collective costs.

## C-028 — Q-009/Q-019

What is the role of finite energy conversion, storage, construction and resource availability in adaptive organisation?

Historical sources: HO-007, HO-014, HO-017.
Related existing IDs: Q-009, Q-019.

Proposed test / boundary: Explicit energy/material ledger, external input, acquisition costs and dissipation, exploit tests; compare supply patterns and metabolic pathways.

<a id="c-029--q-041"></a>
## C-029 — Q-041

Can population bottlenecks, migration, unequal reproduction and catastrophes change adaptive potential independently of population size?

Historical sources: HO-001, HO-005.
Related existing IDs: Q-007, Q-010.

Proposed test / boundary: Effective population size, diversity, pedigree, survival and information loss; replicate bottlenecks; preserve failed lineages.

<a id="c-030--q-042"></a>
## C-030 — Q-042

Can life-like organisation emerge before a programmer-defined agent exists? (Genesis exploratory branch.)

Historical sources: HO-014, HO-017.
Related existing IDs: Q-019.

Proposed test / boundary: Compare metabolism/replication/boundary/regulation starting conditions; define persistent organisation, maintenance, heritable variation and reproduction separately; no claim of abiogenesis.

<a id="c-031--q-043"></a>
## C-031 — Q-043

Can an inherited information-bearing structure both store information and perform a functional role?

Historical sources: HO-001, HO-007, HO-014.
Related existing IDs: H-015, Q-006.

Proposed test / boundary: Compare dual-function structures vs separated controller/storage, matched mutation/replication fidelity and costs. No claim to reproduce RNA chemistry.

<a id="c-032--q-044"></a>
## C-032 — Q-044

How do environment-level reproduction, variation and inheritance affect nested artificial ecosystems?

Historical sources: HO-006, HO-017.
Related existing IDs: Q-010, Q-019.

Proposed test / boundary: Define replicating world units and selection mechanism; compare all worlds, including extinction; no physical universe-creation inference. Long-term exploratory.

<a id="c-033--q-045"></a>
## C-033 — Q-045

Can functional continuity after duplication or restoration be distinguished from structural similarity and subjective identity?

Historical sources: HO-004, HO-005, HO-009.
Related existing IDs: Q-012.

Proposed test / boundary: Genome-only vs complete-state copy vs continuing instance; compare independent trajectories and divergence. No test of metaphysical soul transfer.

<a id="c-034--q-046"></a>
## C-034 — Q-046

What functional indicators and ethical review thresholds are appropriate as capabilities increase?

Historical sources: HO-008, HO-012, HO-014.
Related existing IDs: Q-012.

Proposed test / boundary: Theory-specific indicators, causal self-report controls, periodic ethics review; no conclusive experience score; uncertainty must remain explicit.

<a id="c-035--q-047"></a>
## C-035 — Q-047

What architecture is necessary, sufficient, advantageous or redundant for a specified capability?

Historical sources: HO-001, HO-008, HO-015.
Related existing IDs: H-015.

Proposed test / boundary: Multiple architectures with equivalent information/resources, ablations, competing explanations and predeclared scope; absence of a human-like brain is not proof of architecture independence.

## C-036 — Q-013/Q-016

Can agents generalise to fundamentally unfamiliar environments or benefit from knowledge of biological histories?

Historical sources: HO-001, HO-002, HO-003, HO-005, HO-014.
Related existing IDs: Q-016.

Proposed test / boundary: Held-out environment families, matched baselines and bounded resource costs; predictive model of evolution distinct from artificial evolutionary system.
