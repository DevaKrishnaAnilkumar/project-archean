# Foundational research objectives and standards

The foundational research question concerns elementary cell-like processes, progressively more complex adaptation and persistent information storage. Test these mechanisms independently rather than assuming a trajectory towards human cognition.

**Central question (existing Q-001): Under what environmental and evolutionary conditions does intelligence-like information processing outperform simpler survival strategies?**

Separate three methodologies: (a) retrospective comparative biological forecasting (models *of* evolution); (b) researcher-directed staged capability experiments (current Archean); (c) autonomous heritable variation and ecological selection (M-012). A fourth, exploratory pre-agent research track (exploratory pre-agent organisation) examines the emergence of self-maintaining organisation itself. None is an inevitable linear evolutionary progression; Pre-agent organisation is not a prerequisite for autonomous ecological evolution or a new approved milestone.

## Proposed research standards

1. Evaluate reactivity, regulation, adaptation, memory, learning, generalisation, prediction, planning, coordination, self-modelling and metacognition separately. These are dimensions, not a guaranteed developmental ladder. Survival and viable descendants are ecological outcomes; consciousness is an independent unresolved question.
2. Record each capability as supplied, used, shown advantageous, acquired, selected or structurally emergent. A hand-designed learning mechanism improving outcomes does not establish evolution of learning. A mutation-enabled architecture constrains what may emerge.
3. Specify initial information, external resource flux, full rules, costs, accessible state, replication, mutation and archival boundaries. Audit state leakage, numerical conservation and scheduler artifacts. Researcher-only records are not inherited data.
4. Compare plausible cheap fixed, random, non-evolving, drift and matched alternative mechanisms. Preregister outcomes and scope, retain every designated seed, extinction and negative results. Do not infer inevitability from survivors. Separate hypotheses from external literature and speculation.
5. Preserve original wording, origin, date confidence, subsequent scientific corrections and evidence status. Avoid retroactive authorship claims and treating historical AI-generated explanations as reviewed literature. Separate research provenance from empirical evidence.

## Boundaries

Computational continuity and functional performance are operational constructs; neither demonstrates subjective experience. Historical sources are motivation, not empirical evidence.

## Roadmap recommendation

Keep current milestone sequence M-006–M-014. Candidate directions are a research backlog, not implementation requirements. Prioritize continuous spatial learning (M-006), then heritable plasticity and memory (M-007), life history (M-008), costs/variability (M-009) and bounded structural mutations (M-010). Ecology, social transmission and validated autonomous worlds follow only when earlier controls hold. Pre-agent organisation and environment-level selection are separate exploratory branches. No move to consciousness claims, no change to v2.3.1 data or protocols, no claim of novelty before related-work review.
