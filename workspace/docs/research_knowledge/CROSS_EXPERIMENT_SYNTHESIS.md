No demonstrated logical contradiction between the scoped primary C11 results and earlier records. Null intervals are not equivalence, exploratory outcomes are not confirmations, and apparent compatibility is not a confirmed common causal mechanism.

## 3. Endpoint discipline

Let U be realised living organism-periods / possible slots; F be target organism-periods / realised living organism-periods; G = F_active − mean F_passive in conditional demographic replay; D = U×G for shared realised exposure. D is joint slot enrichment, not a fitness coefficient. F is undefined when there are no realised living periods; per-period frequency is undefined at extinction. Conditional replay fixes the realised birth/death schedule and does not estimate autonomous counterfactual population survival. A population intervention (C11 B-P1) is not individual selection. A difference in G across settings can still reflect exposure duration. Avoid pooling first-choice, conditional decision accuracy, energy, longevity, population abundance, occupancy and frequency into a common effect size. Independent seed blocks, not organisms or replay draws, are inferential units.

## 4. Research gaps and dependencies

1. Replicate C11-B, retaining fixed sensing, zero-mutation and null controls; explore viable matched null settings and positive learner overhead only under separate prospective protocols.
2. Preserve M-014 confirmations of E07 secondary findings. No later model resolves these by substitution.
3. M-017 is a promising next dependency: bounded heritable learning-rate variation against frozen/no-learning and fixed supplied-learning controls; distinguish selection on a parameter from discovery of an algorithm. Require meaningful cost accounting, matched sensing and standing-variation/mutation controls; do not fix a numeric design in this synthesis.
4. M-018 environmental challenges: first calibrate viability and compare constant, fixed schedules and bounded feedback. Do not define difficulty using intelligence outcomes or rescue failing worlds.
5. M-016 reproductive strategies and M-019 guided versus natural selection remain distinct open branches. C10 fixes reproductive mode; guided selection would not show emergence without an explicit objective.
6. Future structural plasticity, resource depletion, competition and held-out transfer require separate validation. Literature novelty and real-world relevance need an independent primary-source review.

## 5. Candidate explanatory framework

Selection may favour adaptive information-processing mechanisms when their realised ecological benefits justify their energetic, temporal and reproductive costs. Stability and informativeness influence value; initial reserves, opportunity to learn, cap and demographics can alter observed selection. Simplification, coexistence, neutral drift, equilibrium and extinction are valid outcomes. This is an evidence-informed hypothesis (H-005/H-007/H-008/H-015), not a universal law, general intelligence claim or proof of spontaneous learning-rule evolution.
