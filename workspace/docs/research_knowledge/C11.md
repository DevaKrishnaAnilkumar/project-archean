## New records and limitations

- F-054: B-P1 fixed learning vs frozen populations, S=1, no mutation; mean total occupancy 95.36% vs 13.30%; 64 vs 15 worlds survived. Population intervention, not an individual causal selection coefficient.
- F-055: B-P2 mixed populations with standing 50/50 L variation, fixed S and no mutation; informative enrichment +42.4589 pp [41.2162,43.7016], null +0.0445 pp [-1.1790,1.2681] (ordinary descriptive 95% intervals). Conditional replay matches actual demographic schedules, not independent survival. All null worlds went extinct; composition is distinct from viability but ecological duration differs. Null monomorphic parity is an implementation invariant, not a discovered effect.
- F-056: B4 minus B3 informative learning-frequency enrichment -1.0157 pp [-2.1274,0.0959], ordinary descriptive interval; inconclusive, not evidence of equivalence or a necessary mutation benefit. See [secondary contrasts](evidence/C11/B_results/secondary_contrasts.csv).

A extinction 0/64 stable cheap, 61/64 unpredictable, 64/64 expensive; B extinction 49/64 frozen informative, zero other informative, 256/256 null. Prior full action/RNG audit retained; second review independently reconstructs demographic ledgers and five primary results, not a second complete action audit.

No additional overhead for learning, no resource depletion, supplied Bayesian update, cap saturation and founder/descendant opportunity differences.
