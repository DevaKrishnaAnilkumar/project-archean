# C09 · Acquired-information retention under survival pressure

Accepted exploratory experiment; 1,200 active worlds, 2,400 conditional passive replays. Twenty independent worlds per setting/population. [Original results](evidence/C09/RESULTS.md), [validation](evidence/C09/VALIDATION.md), [comparison](evidence/C09/CROSS_EXPERIMENT_COMPARISON.md), [branch report](evidence/C09/RESEARCH_BRANCH_CANDIDATES.md), [protocol](../../experiments/c09_acquired_inheritance/PROTOCOL.md).

Primary endpoint counts T>0 occupants of all 28,800 possible population-period slots, not proportion among living organisms. Active and passive share demographic events, so replay is not a counterfactual survival experiment. All extinct worlds retained. Learning and sensing fixed; transmission is offspring confidence shrinkage, not number of memory bits, and donor quality is not a kinship-isolated effect. No novel learning algorithm or reproductive mode evolved.

## Canonical dispositions

- **F-039 — Stable free retention enriches nonblank acquired-information receptivity.** At reward 8 and cheap sensing, +7.14 pp [3.25,11.03] slot occupancy versus passive; stable minus reversal +7.77 pp [4.06,11.49]; stable minus unpredictable +6.98 pp [3.13,10.82]. n=20 paired worlds, exploratory unadjusted intervals. [EXPLORATORY EVIDENCE; evidence](evidence/C09/primary.csv).
- **F-040 — Early offspring accuracy and population abundance can diverge.** Full versus blank under reversal: +7.94 pp first-choice success (original birth cohorts), −7.73 mean organisms. Stable comparison +14.12 pp and +1.77 organisms. Exposure and costs may differ. [EXPLORATORY EVIDENCE; evidence](evidence/C09/RESULTS.md).
- **F-041 — Donor source and environmental persistence change information quality.** Stable parent versus other surviving donor Brier 0.071 vs 0.136; unpredictable 0.372 vs 0.290. Reproductive filtering and survivor sampling confound ancestry; 18 donor fallbacks. [EXPLORATORY EVIDENCE; evidence](evidence/C09/RESULTS.md).
- **F-042 — Partial retention can moderate mismatch after reversal.** Reversal partial vs full newborn Brier 0.192 vs 0.208; blank 0.250. Under unpredictable mapping blank 0.250 vs partial 0.295/full 0.365. Descriptive, not a universal optimum. [EXPLORATORY EVIDENCE; evidence](evidence/C09/alleles.csv).
- **F-043 — Transfer can incur cost without transmitting useful information.** At q=0.50 beliefs stay 0.5 and free blank/full comparable action and energy trajectories match. Costly full transfer spent 7701 units across 20 worlds. This is a model/accounting check; fitness consequences exploratory. [VERIFIED WITHIN SCOPE; evidence](evidence/C09/RESULTS.md).
- **F-044 — Extreme sensing expenditure can overwhelm inherited information.** All 80 extreme cost world-population runs extinct; only five seeds with births in fixed comparisons. Do not infer effect of inheritance from sparse cohorts. [EXPLORATORY EVIDENCE; evidence](evidence/C09/conditions.csv).
- **F-045 — Apparent inherited-belief revision depends on exposure and censoring.** Free reward-8 reversal C: 1071/1237 contradictory newborn beliefs aligned within initial segment before censoring; observed alignments averaged 4.27 periods. Redraw can cause alignment without learning. [EXPLORATORY EVIDENCE; evidence](evidence/C09/RESULTS.md).
- **Q-051 — When do recombination and dual-parent acquired information improve survival?.** Researcher-originated C10; isolate genetic recombination, mutation, pooling, conflicting evidence, mate constraints and energy costs. Neither mode assumed superior. [OPEN QUESTION; evidence](evidence/C09/RESEARCH_BRANCH_CANDIDATES.md).
- **Q-052 — Can reproductive mode itself evolve under survival and demographic pressure?.** Assistant extension explicitly requested for permanent preservation by researcher: fixed single-parent, fixed two-parent, or facultative switching, with costs and demographic controls. Distinct from C10. [OPEN QUESTION; evidence](EVOLVABLE_REPRODUCTION.md).
- **M-015 — C10 controlled recombination and dual-parent information assay.** Prerequisite to separate evolvable reproductive-strategy investigation; new prospective protocol required. [PROPOSED; evidence](evidence/C09/RESEARCH_BRANCH_CANDIDATES.md).
- **M-016 — Evolution of reproductive strategy including facultative switching.** Separate branch, after controlled C10 comparisons and new authorisation. [PROPOSED; evidence](EVOLVABLE_REPRODUCTION.md).
