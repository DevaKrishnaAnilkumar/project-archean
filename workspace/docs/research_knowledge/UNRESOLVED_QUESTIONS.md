# Unresolved discrepancies and open questions

- **Old tuning records.** v1.0 extinction and v1.1 growth are recoverable as README descriptions. Without the original settings and logs, the proposed explanations remain historical conjectures.
- **Arrival frequency versus speed.** The first-contact median increased among successful starts while reach improved. Revised analysis must retain failed starts and distinguish routes from changes in the successful set.
- **Fixed learning and action.** C07 supports exact action parity for fixed learning under a one-reading, equal-prior comparator. Multiple readings or a confidence-dependent action set could break parity. The current calculations cannot infer the corresponding survival change.
- **Belief and performance.** C07's overall accuracy ranking and the reversal recovery metric address different outcomes and time windows. A later locked analysis should declare post-reversal calibration, action utility and recovery separately.
- **Exact versus finite samples.** C02 intervals and C03 exact gains coexist. C04 original-cost, C06 fixed-versus-planner and C07 unpredictable comparisons are not grounds for claiming unconditional equivalence. Explicitly distinguish exact ties in the specified model from sampling uncertainty.
- **Risk avoidance.** C06 makes finite abstention safe. C07 continues maintenance but requires affordable attempts. An optional, repeated abstention or informed-risk comparison remains an open design question.
- **Bottleneck and inheritance.** Existing results give no causal lineage advantage attributable to learned state rather than founding weights, energy-based retention, designed copying, or scheduler order.
- **Accounting and paid cognition.** Closed-form trip value assuming full traversal does not apply directly to survivor-selected trips, deaths or no-attempt periods. Planning and learning costs need an explicitly declared accounting rule.
