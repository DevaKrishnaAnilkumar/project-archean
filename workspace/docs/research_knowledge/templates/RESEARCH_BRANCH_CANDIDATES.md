# Research branch candidates

## Candidate heading

1. Originating experiment and evidence pointer.
2. Original observation or recovered statement, with exact quote versus paraphrase.
3. Interpretation.
4. Origin of idea and attribution confidence.
5. Scientific classification.
6. Competing explanations.
7. Relevant existing register entries.
8. Proposed test.
9. Dependencies and limitations.
10. Current execution status.

Link immediate and earlier sources. Record negative, null and unexpected outcomes regardless of primary relevance. Use temporary descriptive headings until the planning conversation assigns permanent IDs.
