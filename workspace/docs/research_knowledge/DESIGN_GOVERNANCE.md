# Research design extensions

## Benchmark headroom and matched controls

The AS-P01 decision tree resolves a small information-gap case. AS-P02/C05/C06 implement strong fixed and random comparators and exact dynamic programming in specified assays. Before scaling replication, check the exact or approximate room for an action difference. Separate entropy, action accuracy, and experienced survival. For a matched benchmark, report what information each policy gets and what sensing, storage, inference and movement cost it pays. A fixed baseline optimised for each setting and a fixed baseline selected before transfer answer different questions.

Use the proposed validation sequence when appropriate. L0 validates implementation. L1 evaluates controlled comparisons. L2 broadens environmental parameters. L3 focuses on prespecified extremes. L4 independently confirms a locked prediction with fresh seeds. Seed count is an evaluation choice. Environmental variables and heritably mutable traits need separate specifications. The sequence is a planning proposal linked to Q-001/Q-004/Q-015/H-007. C05 did not estimate the real-world prevalence of grid outcomes.

## Variability and lifetime investment

Complexity may rise, stagnate or decline with changing benefits. Moderate, learnable pressures could favour investment when generous or extreme regimes favour simpler strategies. Monotonic, threshold, multiple-peak and null relationships remain competitors. Independently vary predictability, change magnitude, resource supply, reserves, opportunity to learn and cognitive costs. Include zero-cost ablations and neutral or drift controls when testing heritable mechanisms. Link H-005–H-008/Q-003/Q-004/Q-010/Q-032/M-007/M-009. These are hypotheses and proposed designs.

Reserve savings could matter for later survival or viable descendants even where completed-trip probability ties. C07's ongoing maintenance motivates the idea, but C06 has only one trip and C07 has no reproduction or adaptive reserve-preserving planner. Link Q-007/H-010 and test under continuous budgets before studying descendants. A related distinction between learning a sensor's reliability and learning whether to sense. At stable eight units and 2.5 cost, the oracle averaged 4.880625 periods alive, below the no-sensing mean 6.970; the enforced sampling rule applies to both ordinary sensing policies and the oracle. See C07 condition means. Reserve-dependent cognition and optional sensing are separate proposed tests.

## Founding constraints and comparative research

The scientific formulation treats functional capacity as a possible consequence of survival and reproduction, with simplification, equilibrium and extinction valid outcomes. Human cognition is comparative context.

A proposed specialisation is selection for acquiring, trusting, revising, forgetting and transmitting knowledge. Design mechanisms and costs explicitly. An inherited-state arm needs a declared source of parental state, common genotype controls, unrelated and empty-state controls and independent replicated worlds. Compare passive transmission and selection. Link H-008/H-009/Q-006/Q-029/M-007/M-009. This is a candidate programme.

Compare hidden simulated sources using yoked exposure, independent perturbations and forecasting under held-out conditions. Separate detecting a change, predicting it and identifying an action-contingent process. Classify unexpected behaviour as candidate adaptation, permitted interaction with simulated rules, measurement artefact or implementation defect. A simulator defect goes through correction and revalidation. Evidence of modelled action contingency cannot establish awareness or identify causes outside the simulated system. All causal tests would operate solely on simulated variables within the declared action set.

Compare genealogy, neutral/drift controls, computational costs, held-out ecologies and independent-world replication after verifying primary sources. Full Sasaki–Tokoro methods were unavailable in the cited discussion. Claims of novelty or superiority require a separately sourced study.
