## Findings and interpretation

- **Recombination changes reachable genotype combinations.** Complementary founders L0S1 (genotype 2) and L1S0 (genotype 3) have no joint L1S1 (genotype 5). Without mutation, paired clone blank generated no genotype 5 in any of the 20 stable worlds, while paired recombination blank generated genotype 5 in all 20, including 240 births with premutation genotype 5 from parents neither carrying genotype 5. Its mean slot occupancy was 11.09%. This is a supplied two-locus recombination mechanism, not de novo cognitive architecture. With 2% mutation the incremental contrast was +9.91 pp. Under 10% mutation stable, the secondary contrast was +5.98 pp [4.51,7.45]; mutation and recombination were independently varied. In the balanced-founder condition the contrast was +1.05 pp [−1.10,+3.19]. These descriptive setting differences are not prespecified interaction tests.
- **Genetic novelty does not establish adaptive intelligence.** In the uninformative setting q=.50, recombination blank vs paired clone blank still increased joint occupancy by +8.43 pp [6.66,10.20] (secondary); all beliefs stayed .5. This is consistent with combinatorial generation, not evidence that the joint genotype was selected for useful learning. No passive replay was specified for C10. Compare F-032/F-039 only with explicit scope limitations.
- **Population capacity masks some information outcomes.** Pooled and primary parental memories had exactly identical total population time series across all 20 stable and all 20 reversal pairs at reward 12. However, genotype time series differed in 39/40 pairs. Stable pooled memory improved first-choice success over observable birth cohorts by +1.76 pp [0.61,2.91] and reduced newborn Brier error by 0.00374 [−0.00598,−0.00149] (secondary). Thus equal demographic occupancy does not mean identical behaviour. A cap near saturation limits this endpoint's sensitivity; the result is not proof of equivalence in other environments.
- **Low-resource pooling is a candidate follow-up, not a confirmed general benefit.** At stable reward 8, cheap sensing, pair recombination pooled vs primary memory increased total occupancy by +4.45 pp [0.45,8.45], corresponding to mean populations 43.95 vs 38.61 (secondary, unadjusted). Both arms had four extinct worlds. First-choice contrast +1.00 pp [−0.40,+2.39]; newborn Brier difference +0.01352 [−0.00361,+0.03064] (higher is worse). Possible mechanisms include reduced overconfidence, changed behavioural pathways, genotype/donor exposure and demographic variation. Conflicting beliefs, conditional donor quality and correlated evidence are not independently manipulated; do not claim pooling resolves conflict or establishes a causal mediation mechanism. Record future prospective verification.
- **Pair requirement and shared investment have competing consequences.** In the small-founder setting, paired clone blank mean population 99.53 vs single blank 105.91, with 1/20 vs 0/20 extinctions. P5 interval crosses zero. The arms match total reproduction energy cost but differ in partner requirement and per-parent debit; cannot isolate mate scarcity or cost sharing. Under the normal stable low-reward setting pair clone blank had 8 extinct worlds vs single blank 14; no simple mode ranking. No spatial mate search, sex roles or diploidy.
- **Energetic limits persist across modes.** All 160 high-sensing-cost worlds (8 arms ×20 seeds) became extinct. Sparse birth and learning exposure limits information-transfer comparisons. Retain them in denominators.
- **Null and structural checks.** At q=.50 all matched memory-only arms had byte-identical raw ledgers across 20 seeds within each genetic/reproduction mode. The complementary zero-mutation clone arms cannot combine learning and sensing; without learning from sensed evidence, birth beliefs remain blank. All birth events transferred 12 energy units and dissipated one additional unit, with one or two distinct eligible parents depending on mode. All raw records, including genotype transitions and extinct trajectories, are preserved.
