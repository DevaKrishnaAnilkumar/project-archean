# C10 cumulative evidence comparison

| Earlier record(s) | Relationship | C10 observation and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| F-001, Q-009, Q-041, F-036 | EXTENDS | Birth cap, energy continuity and extinction retained; near-cap abundance can mask memory effects. The scarce founder setting changes both initial count and reserve. Original v1 runs unavailable. |
| F-032, F-033, H-005/H-008 | QUALIFIES | More L1S1 combinations can be generated with uninformative observations. C10 recombination contrast is not a conditional neutral selection comparison. Novel genotype frequency alone does not show adaptive learning. |
| F-038 | EXTENDS | Separate possible-slot occupancy from realised living frequency; stable/reversal pooled and primary total occupancy equal but genotypes diverge. |
| F-007, F-039–F-042 | EXTENDS | Two-parent arithmetic pooling of acquired beliefs gives context-dependent outcomes. Stable high reward: modest calibration and first-choice gains but same population trace. Low reward: exploratory occupancy advantage with no clear early choice or Brier improvement. Different donor mechanism; no isolation of kinship or conflict. |
| F-026, F-040 | EXTENDS | Beliefs, choice and demography need not rank strategies alike. Equal abundance does not imply equal genotype trajectories. |
| F-027/F-028/F-034/F-044 | SUPPORTS | All C10 expensive-sensing worlds extinct; cannot establish universal threshold. F-028 full-trip energy bound remains scoped. |
| Q-051/M-015 | EXTENDS | Controlled single/pair, clone/recombination and blank/primary/pool arms executed. Costs of mate search and varying partner availability, explicit opposing experiences, recombination architectures and evolving conflict resolution remain open. |
| Q-052/M-016, H-018 | NOT COMPARABLE | Reproductive strategy is fixed by arm; no adaptive waiting policy. Preserve these separate open branches. |
