## Researcher-originated C10 candidate

**C10 · Recombination and dual-parent information inheritance.** Source is the researcher's direction received during C09 closeout. C08, C09, H-009, H-010, Q-006 and Q-008 provide the research ancestry. This entry describes an untested extension and leaves C09 unchanged.

Investigate two-parent reproduction as an alternative to single-parent reproduction. Separate genetic recombination from mutation and from dual-parent transmission of acquired information. Vary whether parents contribute genes, memory, both or neither, and compare agreeing versus conflicting parental knowledge. Keep selective retention and conflict-resolution mechanisms distinguishable. Measure whether any supplied or evolvable conflict-resolution rule improves outcomes without giving it direct fitness credit.

Test reproductive costs, mate search and mate availability separately from recombination and information. Include matched single-parent baselines under stable, reversing and unpredictable environments, with extreme scarcity and population bottlenecks. Ask whether joint genetic traits and experiences improve viability or reproduction, or when single-parent reproduction is superior because partners are scarce, delays and costs are high, or pooling degrades useful beliefs. Include neutral or negligible effects as possible outcomes. Preregister controls and separate ecological persistence, offspring viability and information calibration. The specific design, implementation and execution remain pending review.
