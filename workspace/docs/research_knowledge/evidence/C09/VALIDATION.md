# Validation, deviations and limitations

C08 checkpoint, clean baseline, object integrity and closeout archive SHA-256 matched before locking the protocol. The approved study ran on Linux x86_64, Python 3.12.14, NumPy 2.3.5, Matplotlib 3.10.8 and GCC 14.2.0. Full dependency and executable provenance are in provenance/ENVIRONMENT.txt.

Smoke covered all 15 settings, four populations and two seeds. It ran 120 active worlds and 240 replays within the 2 GiB cap, projecting 0.78 GB raw evidence for evaluation. The single-threaded evaluation, audit and reporting sequence ran in 31.090 seconds under the 900-second watchdog. It generated 1,200 active worlds, 2,400 conditional replays and 780,208,358 raw bytes. All 39 earlier tests passed.

Audit independently reconstructed recorded births, donor identity and donor source, all retention values, mutation, exact energy, irreversible death, newborn timing, pedigrees and random passive replay. The inherited source kernel's visibility regression was preserved and sampled action draws additionally reconstructed. Free null-cue A/B parity passed for all 20 evaluation seeds. A second event-delta reconstruction checked 2,700 active and passive occupancy values with zero discrepancy, and all three primary contrasts and intervals within 1e-14. No substantive result or protocol deviations were observed.

Initial compilation exposed GCC's non-main implicit-return warning when making the archived C08 main inert. The C09 wrapper scoped that warning to the historical include and left C08 source unchanged. The locked model was rebuilt byte-identically before evaluation. Revision timings are descriptive and reflect observed rather than latent beliefs. The offspring duration table right-censors at the horizon, and separate first-choice columns preserve the original birth denominators. Secondary intervals may have fewer than 20 matched pairs when the outcome is undefined with no births. All bounds on C06/C07 energetic effects retain their original scope.
