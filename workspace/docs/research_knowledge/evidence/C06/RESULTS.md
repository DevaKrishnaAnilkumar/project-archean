## Scientific findings

- Under task completion first, exact adaptive planning tied the best fixed sequence on completion probability in all 108 settings. It improved completion over myopic exploration in six settings. The largest gain was 2.1055 percentage points at four energy units, free sensing and half reliability.
- At four energy units, one-quarter sensing cost and full reliability, task-first adaptive and fixed policies both completed 80.87% of trips in exact expectation. Myopic completed 78.91%. Adaptive used 2.205 expected visits versus 3 for fixed. Simulation estimated +2.5625 percentage points for adaptive versus myopic, with an exploratory 95% paired interval of [+2.0589, +3.0661] points. Adaptive versus fixed was −0.2188 points, interval [−0.6099, +0.1724], with an exact difference of zero.
- At four energy units, every survival-first policy abstained in every exact setting. They survived the episode with certainty, completed no trips and acquired no resources. Adaptive survival and completion differed between the two objectives at all 18 four-unit settings. At all 90 settings with at least eight units, the two objectives gave identical exact adaptive survival and completion probabilities.
- With eight initial units and the highest cost multiplier, a compulsory sensing trip spent five units. The three-unit reserve could reach the resource but depended on a reward to return. At full reliability, task-first compulsory entropy seeking had 74.5% exact survival, compared with 100% for immediate travel. With uninformative cues its exact survival fell to 50%.
- Adaptive planning improved expected final energy over the best fixed sequence in 14 task-first settings while tying its completion and survival. Under task-first priorities it could also spend more expected energy than the myopic rule in exchange for completing more trips. At 64 energy the exact myopic and adaptive energy expectations matched their C05 counterparts across all costs, reliabilities and both objectives.

Intervals are exploratory and reflect seed-level uncertainty. The exact values are expectations for the supplied simulation. The experiment supports no claims about evolution, reproduction, lifetime learning, computational cost of planning or survival beyond the finite episode.
