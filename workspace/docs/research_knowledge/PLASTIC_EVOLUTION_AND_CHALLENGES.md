# Plastic evolution and escalating environmental challenges

## Provenance

Researcher-originated proposal, 28 November 2026:
compare evolving learning mechanisms in artificial-life agents
and test fixed or adaptive environmental challenge schedules.
Whether these favour adaptive capabilities is an open hypothesis.

AI-assisted experimental formulations distinguish lifetime
plasticity, heritable mechanisms, escalating environments,
and explicit capability objectives.

Original provenance remains in the private archive.

## Q-053 / M-017: Lifetime structural plasticity

Compare frozen controllers, weight-plastic controllers,
heritable architecture and bounded lifetime structural
reorganisation. Separate growth, pruning, recurrent memory,
connection changes and computational costs.

Larger networks may improve adaptation, be redundant,
consume excessive energy or reduce reproductive success.

## Q-054 / M-017: Evolution of learning rules

Allow heritable learning-rate modifiers and eventually
bounded alternative plasticity rules. Distinguish learning
during life from genetic evolution across generations.

Compare fixed rules, evolved parameters and evolved
mechanisms under matched information and energy budgets.
Preserve simpler successful solutions.

## Q-055 / M-017: Evolving evolutionary operators

Investigate heritable mutation-rate modifiers and
recombination parameters independently of reproductive
mode. Test standing variation, linkage, mutation load,
extinction, lineage persistence and functional adaptation.

Link Q-030, Q-051 and Q-052. Do not assume more mutations
or recombination produce better cognition.

## Q-056 / M-018: Progressive environmental challenge

Compare constant, linear and bounded exponential schedules.
A candidate schedule is D(t) = min(Dmax, D0 * exp(k*t)).

Difficulty and intelligence are distinct variables.
Increasing challenge may favour learning, simpler rapid
reproduction, equilibrium or extinction.

Calibrate environmental feasibility independently.
Separate cue reliability, resource scarcity, hazard severity,
variability and energetic costs.

## Q-057 / M-018: Adaptive challenge feedback

Compare predetermined schedules with bounded,
predeclared population-performance feedback.
Include fixed and matched open-loop controls.
Avoid researcher rescue, hidden information leakage
and post-hoc adjustment to favour desired outcomes.

A population-performance trigger is not necessarily
an intelligence trigger.

## Q-058 / M-019: Natural versus guided evolution

Natural branch: selection through survival and reproduction.
Guided branch: explicitly defined additional capability
objective, reported as a distinct selection mechanism.

Compare held-out adaptation, energetic efficiency,
viable descendants, generalisation and complexity.
Match evaluation and computational budgets.

An explicit intelligence criterion cannot demonstrate
that intelligence arose without such a criterion.

## Dependencies and boundaries

Extend H-015/M-010 and Q-005 rather than replacing them.
Connect environmental schedules to Q-010/M-012.
Retain Q-052/M-016 as the separate reproductive-strategy
evolution branch.

Polyworld and POET are related-work starting points.
Literature and novelty claims require a verified review.

All proposals require separate protocols, validation,
scientific authorisation and computational limits.
No assumption of exponential intelligence growth,
consciousness or open-ended evolution is established.
