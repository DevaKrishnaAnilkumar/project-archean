# C08 · Selection on supplied learning and sensing

C08 is complete and has passed scoped independent scientific review. Research planning reported reconstructing the primary measures across 800 active worlds and 6,400 passive replays with zero discrepancy. That is a reconstruction of existing raw outcomes. Scientific inference remains exploratory. [Protocol](../../experiments/c08_heritable_learning/PROTOCOL.md) · [Prior evidence](../../experiments/c08_heritable_learning/PRIOR_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md) · [Locked analysis](../../experiments/c08_heritable_learning/ANALYSIS.md) · [Execution audit](evidence/C08/evaluation-audit.log).

## Denominators and scope

The primary measure is **population-slot occupancy**. Sum end-of-period living organisms with L=1 and S>0, including newborns, and divide by 120 × 240 = 28,800 possible slots. Empty slots and extinct periods contribute zero. Subtract the mean of eight neutral demographic replays of the same birth and death timing. **Genotype frequency among living organisms** divides a genotype's realised living organism-periods by all realised living organism-periods. End-period frequency uses living counts at that period. Composition is undefined at zero living count. Within each world, replay holds occupancy opportunity constant. Across settings, extinction and lower abundance constrain the magnitude of possible occupancy differences. The paired metric cannot assess a different population's counterfactual survival.

C08 has six supplied genotypes and passes on genotype alone. Newborns begin with blank beliefs. It does not test acquired-state inheritance, new learning algorithms, evolving planning or risk avoidance, or ecological interactions beyond birth-capacity constraints. Resources are independent encounters. Reward size is an energy credit and changes neither food density nor depletion.

## Outcomes and branches

| Record | Observation, provenance and next test |
|---|---|
| F-032 · Stable cheap joint enrichment | At reward 8 and zero overhead, occupancy was 47.62% active versus 16.84% passive, difference +30.78 points [25.60, 35.96]. Matched enrichment contrasts were +30.10 points [25.01, 35.19] versus unpredictable cheap and +30.87 [25.70, 36.04] versus stable expensive. In a different denominator, L1 S1 occupied 85.05% of living organism-periods versus 15.41% passive. Its realised accuracy and births per exposure exceeded L0 S1. C08 evidence tests the researcher's H-005/H-008 hypothesis within scope. Chance reward, ancestry, mutation and turnover can contribute to divergence. Proposed fresh-seed confirmation with fixed-composition and mutation controls requires authorisation. |
| F-033 · Unpredictable regimes separate sensing from learning | Cheap reward-8 unpredictable worlds had +0.68 points joint occupancy difference and 49.9% learning prevalence among realised living periods. L1 S1 mapping Brier was 0.287 versus 0.250 for L0 S1, while choice accuracy was 68.08% versus 67.65%. Current cues can retain value without persistent mapping. Demography and S-specific selection can explain joint enrichment. Extends F-029 and H-006. A future autocorrelation sweep could isolate immediate and learned information. |
| F-034 · Cost and persistence | All stable reward-8 expensive worlds died out even though L1 S1 chose correctly on 77.65% of its observed attempts. In reward-12 unpredictable expensive worlds with overhead, all persisted and no-sensing genotypes accounted for 67.3% of living periods. Earlier C05/C06/C07 relationships are cost, survival and accuracy. C08's separate post-hoc full-trip bound is reward × (0.15 + 0.70 × accuracy) − 5 − fee − overhead before reproduction. Reward-8 break-even is 73.44% at fee 0.3125 and 112.5% at fee 2.5 with zero overhead. Return mortality, affordability and birth costs limit generalisation. A new near-threshold design could test the cost transition with fresh worlds. |
| F-035 · Null cues and unused overhead | At q=0.50 mapping beliefs stayed blank. Nonsensing L1 S0 still paid overhead where specified. Joint depletion was −2.60 points with no overhead [−5.61, +0.40], and −7.46 [−10.30, −4.63] with overhead. The belief and charging rules are model-scoped checks. Composition is exploratory and can reflect drift or unequal exposure. Extends F-031 and Q-004. A future factorial assay could split idle and realised update costs. |
| F-036 · Abundance, cap and founding lineages | Raising stable cheap reward from 8 to 12 at zero overhead raised mean abundance 64.1 to 119.4 and represented founder lines at the horizon 4.20 to 51.10, while enrichment fell from +30.78 to +9.72 points. With free stable reward-8 sensing, enrichment rose to +45.58 points without overhead and +42.83 with it. Turnover, longevity and birth-slot blocking are candidate explanations. Cap itself was held fixed. Extends F-001, Q-007 and Q-041. A separate authorised study could vary cap and reward with matched demographies. |
| F-037 · Longevity and realised reproduction diverge | In zero-overhead free stable sensing L1 S1 averaged 61.67 observed action periods versus 34.91 for L0 S1, but 1.63 versus 2.05 births per 100 at-risk periods. Capacity blocking, age, timing and horizon censoring can shift these denominators. Extends F-025 and Q-007. A future opportunity-level decomposition needs births, eligibility and open slots. |
| Q-050 · Feedback exposure | Early reserve and feedback opportunities remain competing mechanisms for initial collapse. C08's affordability gate does not test H-018's waiting hypothesis. AI discussion originated H-018 and the researcher selected it for follow-up. A prospective contrast could control early exposures without excluding extinct worlds. |
| F-038 · Limits of conditional replay | The occupancy bound in a world is its realised filled-slot fraction, so low abundance compresses the possible active-minus-passive difference. This follows from the metric and shared counts. Composition, persistence and abundance remain distinct targets. Related to F-001, Q-009 and H-005. A future protocol should separate these endpoints before execution. |
