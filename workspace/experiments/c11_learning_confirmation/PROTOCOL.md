## 4. B: mechanism extension

Eight settings: cross stable q=.85 (informative) and q=.50 (null) with four population configurations, reward 8, fee=.3125, overhead 0, S=1 invariant. B1 all 60 L0, mutation disabled; B2 all 60 L1, mutation disabled; B3 30 L0 + 30 L1, randomly permuted, no mutation; B4 same mixed founders and order as B3, only L flips independently at .02 per birth. S never mutates. Fixed populations survive independently under their respective policies, not conditional demographic clones. Eight matched conditional neutral replays per B3 and B4 active world, same L-only or zero mutation and same roster order; no replays for monomorphic B1/B2. Same C08 environment, energy, founder IDs, schedules and random opportunity keys across B arms within seed; descendant IDs are not individually matched biological units. At q=.50 and zero overhead, expect exact equality of B1/B2 mapping, actions (including beliefs), energy, births/deaths, IDs and occupancy ignoring genotype labels; this is a deterministic validation invariant, not a statistical discovery. Mixed null populations can still drift, and individual seed replay contrasts need not vanish. If invariant fails, stop and investigate rather than reinterpret as evidence.

Proposed n=64 seed blocks distinct from A: 4000–4063 inclusive, smoke 3990/3991; same historical-seed check as A. 512 active worlds and 2048 passive replays. B has no directly applicable historical paired variance; n=64 is a bounded design choice matching A, not a demonstrated power guarantee. For B-P1 paired fraction differences in [-1,1], worst-case conventional 97.5% t interval half-width approximately 29 pp; B-P2 difference of two enrichment fractions can span [-2,2], so worst-case precision is poorer. Wide intervals are inconclusive; no sample expansion after outcomes. No claim of equivalence based on nonsignificance. ## 5. Endpoints and prospective statistics

All quantities as fractions unless multiplied by 100 for percentage points. Per world O = sum over end-period L1 S>0 counts / 28800, U = sum living counts / 28800, F = sum target genotype counts / sum living counts (realised organism-period frequency). Newborns included. Empty periods zero occupancy; frequency undefined at empty periods, do not impute. Integrated F is defined for these locked conditions because founders necessarily survive first period; if violated treat as validation failure. Replay D = O_active - mean(O_passive); G = F_active - mean(F_passive), where identical realised denominator in each active/replay set implies D=U*G. In B all S=1 so target is L1. Record end-period frequency only when alive, pooled and equally world-weighted frequency distinctly. No conditioning primary survival outcomes on persistence.

A primary estimands per seed: A-P1 D(A1); A-P2 D(A1)-D(A2); A-P3 D(A1)-D(A3). Report equal-weight seed means, sample SD, ordinary 95% two-sided paired t intervals and simultaneous Bonferroni intervals using t(63, 1-.05/(2*3)) (98.333% each). A-P1 principal; confirm positive enrichment if adjusted lower bound >0, practically meaningful if >.05. A-P2/A-P3 positive contextual contrasts if respective adjusted lower bounds >0; all three must pass directional criterion to describe all original primary contrasts as replicated. Effects with upper bound <0 support opposite direction; intervals crossing zero inconclusive as to direction. Within [-.05,.05] adjusted interval supports effects bounded by proposed practical margin; margin chosen for planning (5% of possible slots), not biological universal constant. Compare historical magnitude descriptively; no formal equivalence to C08 or post hoc pooling.

B primary estimands per seed: B-P1 U(B2 informative)-U(B1 informative), independent ecological viability effect; B-P2 G(B3 informative)-G(B3 null), learning enrichment under useful vs useless information with no mutation and fixed sensing. Use ordinary 95% and 97.5% Bonferroni two-sided t intervals (two primary comparisons). Directional evidence if adjusted lower bound >0; practical evidence if >.05 (five pp occupancy or realised frequency respectively, distinct estimands). For B-P2 also require positive informative G point estimate for interpretation as enrichment, not merely less depletion; report its unadjusted interval as secondary. No claim B-P1 proves individual causal advantage; population-level intervention, opportunity matching and demographic feedback differ. B-P2 conditional null is not survival counterfactual. A and B constitute separate prespecified inferential families (no global 5% claim). B analyses planned prospectively but mechanistic extension lacks independent replication. No joint success criterion forcing both B effects positive. Secondary: G and D separately by condition; U, horizon persistence and extinction period, population and lineage curves, each L/S genotype, mutation comparison [G(B4 informative)-G(B3 informative)] and null counterpart, genotype-specific outcomes, accuracy conditional on attempted choices, realised sensing, Brier scores, resource intake and expenditure, eligible/blocked birth rates, founder lines and longevity censoring. Report all regardless of sign with denominators; secondary intervals descriptive, not confirmatory. Matched binary extinction risk differences with per-seed descriptive uncertainty; horizon censor at 240; no dropping extinct seeds. No individual-level pseudo-replication. Optional deterministic leave-one-seed-out influence summary and nonparametric bootstrap only if algorithm, replicate count and analysis RNG fixed before lock; otherwise omit. No post hoc exclusions, optional stopping or rescue. Missing or corrupt outputs fail validation; technical rerun only exact locked settings and seed with original failure preserved and disclosed, never replacement seed after outcomes.
