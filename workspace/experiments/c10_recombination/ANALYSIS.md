# C10 locked analysis

Independent unit = world. End-of-period counts include newborns; empty slots and extinct periods zero. Population occupancy = sum living / (120*240). Joint genotype occupancy = sum gt5 / (120*240). Living frequency divides by realised living organism-periods (undefined if zero). Five exploratory primary paired seed-level differences (20 seeds, sample SD, two-sided t95 CI 19df, unadjusted):

1. Setting 3 (stable, no mutation, complementary): arm 5 minus 2 joint occupancy (recombination vs clone in paired blank populations).
2. Setting 0 (stable, mutation .02): arm 5 minus 2 joint occupancy.
3. Setting 0: arm 7 minus 6 population occupancy (pool vs primary memory with recombination).
4. Setting 1: arm 7 minus 6 population occupancy.
5. Setting 9 (scarce founders): arm 2 minus 0 population occupancy (pair vs single blank, combined mate/cost-sharing effect).

No directional support threshold or multiplicity adjustment. Report all effects, CIs crossing zero are inconclusive, not equivalence. Descriptive secondary: extinction/persistence, birth counts, death counts, cumulative genotype richness, joint genotype emergence (first birth/period), time-averaged living genotype frequencies, population trajectories, eligible counts and birth-blocked/unpaired opportunities, first-action resource accuracy over original observable newborn cohort (also conditional attempt accuracy), birth belief Brier relative to next period mapping, parent disagreement abs(p1-p2) and opposite-side frequency (strict opposite side of .5), energy spent and per-parent costs, mutation and premutation recombinant genotype counts. Compare null-memory parity. Secondary paired contrasts on occupancy, joint occupancy, first accuracy, birth belief Brier for arm comparisons 2-0, 5-2, 7-6, 4-3, 1-0, across all settings; undefined cohort outcomes excluded only from that contrast with matched n reported. Complete per-world data retained. At-horizon births excluded only from next-period outcome denominators. Exploratory subgroup descriptions not causal. No fitness claim from accuracy alone or from conditional frequencies. No post-run parameter tuning.
