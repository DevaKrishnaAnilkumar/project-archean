C10 addresses Q-051/M-015. Q-052/M-016 (evolution of reproductive strategy) remains a separate proposal.

| Evidence | Design implication |
|---|---|
| F-001, Q-009, Q-041 | Preserve extinction, birth-blocking cap, conserved transfers, no energy-ranked culling. Include a small, low-energy founder condition. Earlier v1 configurations remain unavailable. |
| F-005, F-025–F-026, F-029–F-031 | Retain C08 sensory kernel, varying stable/reversing/unpredictable mappings; distinguish calibration from actions and survival; include uninformative control. |
| F-019–F-024, F-027–F-028, F-034 | Include cheap and expensive sensing and low reward. Fixed foraging/sensing affordability rules do not resolve evolved risk avoidance H-018. |
| F-032–F-038, H-005, H-008 | Reuse L/S traits. Recombination can bring together complementary founder alleles; all six genotypes already present in a balanced-founder control. Occupancy and living frequency have separate denominators. Do not attribute fixed-mode differences to selection versus drift without a neutral benchmark. |
| F-007, H-009, F-039–F-045 | Parent information may help, become stale or differ from another donor. Compare blank, primary-parent and pooled parental beliefs. Arithmetic pooling avoids treating shared experience as independent Bayesian evidence. Examine conflicting beliefs, calibration, first choices, abundance and reproduction independently. |
| H-010, Q-006–Q-008, Q-018, Q-051 | Record both parents, total and per-parent energy transfers, mate availability. Match pairing and expenditure between genetic recombination controls; single versus pair contrasts still combine mate requirement and cost sharing. |
| Q-052/M-016 | Do not implement an evolving reproductive-mode allele or facultative switching here. |

C10 can show effects of imposed reproductive modes in this model, not spontaneous emergence of reproduction, new cognitive architecture or open-ended intelligence. No food depletion or physical mating search; C08/C09 independent encounters continue. The controlled comparison is exploratory, not a fresh replication of C08 or C09.
