# C10 — Controlled reproduction, recombination and dual-parent experience

## Scope and question
Prospectively specified exploratory controlled assay, 27 November 2026. Does requiring two parents, recombining supplied loci, or pooling two parents' acquired beliefs improve persistence or generate adaptive genotype combinations? Competing possibilities include disrupted combinations, shared/stale information, null effects and extinction. No cognitive reward. All conditions retained. ## Dynamics
Reuse C08 C++ foraging kernel unchanged. Integer energy ticks = 1/16 unit, horizon 240, cap 120. Normal founders 60 at 24 units. Small/scarce condition 12 at 8 units. Founder beliefs .5. Two loci L={0 frozen,1 Bayesian update with hazard .05}, S={0,.5,1 sensing probability}; genotype 3L+2S. Frozen individuals retain their birth belief rather than reset it (blank birth = .5). Each period maintenance 1 unit, no learning overhead. Single optional affordability-gated sensor visit, C08 exact reward and travel rules, irreversible death at <=0. World mapping stable, switches every 24 periods, or independent redraw. Individual hidden resource states independent, good/bad reward probabilities .85/.15. q=.85 or .50. Resource round trip 4 units. Birth only after surviving adults complete period; no newborn acts until next period. No rescue, food depletion or direct competition except cap. Stable resource rewards 8/12 are energetic credits, not resource density.

## Founder controls
Complementary founders: equal numbers gt2 (frozen, always sense) and gt3 (learns, never sense); joint gt5 absent. Balanced founder control: all six genotypes equally represented. All founder order randomised via C08 founder stream. Recombination may generate joint gt5 without mutation; mutation can also generate it when enabled. No new alleles outside supplied loci. Full baseline L/S diversity exists only in balanced control.

## Reproduction and cost matching
Surviving adults with at least 32 energy eligible. Shuffle eligible indices with C08 reproduction stream keyed by period. Single-parent: sequential eligible adults each produce at most one child while capacity allows; deduct 13 units, child receives 12. Pair: consecutive disjoint eligible pairs in same random ordering, first is primary, second partner; require two distinct eligible adults. Each contributes 6.5 units, one child receives 12, total reproductive loss 1 unit as in single-parent. A participant cannot contribute to another birth that period. No birth or debit if cap blocks; odd unmatched adult cannot reproduce. Report eligible counts, births and unpaired eligible adults, with capacity-blocked counts separate. Single versus pair comparison includes both mate requirement and cost sharing; not pure cost or sex effect. No search/mating preferences, mating types or diploidy.

## Arms (8)
0 single clone blank; 1 single clone primary memory; 2 pair clone blank; 3 pair clone primary memory; 4 pair clone pooled memory; 5 pair recombination blank; 6 pair recombination primary memory; 7 pair recombination pooled memory.
Clone genotype is primary's. Recombination independently samples each L and S locus from either parent (fair coin), separate stream 14 keyed by child ID. Mutation after inheritance uses unchanged C08 per-locus mutation rule and stream. Memory is .5, primary's post-action belief, or arithmetic mean of both parents' post-action beliefs; no extra transfer cost in this assay. In pair clone arms, partner still contributes energy (and in pooled arm memory). Recombination comparisons match pairing and energy transfer. Pooling comparisons match pairing, genetics and mutation; offspring exposures may diverge after intervention. Track both parent identities and beliefs, premutation and postmutation genotype, newborn belief and energy debits. For frozen offspring memory is fixed; this is a supplied feature, not learning. No inheritance of environmental true state or unobserved outcome.
