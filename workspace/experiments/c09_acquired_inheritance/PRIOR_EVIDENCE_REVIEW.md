Mutation and bottlenecks can imitate allele changes. |

A positive result is scoped to a supplied retention rule. Alternatives include confidence mismatch after reversals, conditional donor survival, transfer costs, cap and extinction effects, mostly random choice with little useful transmission, or no measurable enrichment.
