# C08 locked analysis and implementation conventions

27 November 2026 · 40 settings × 20 evaluation world seeds · exploratory

Use signed integer 1/16-unit energy and preserve the C07 sequence of three sensing charges (2/5, 1/5, 2/5), four outward half-unit steps, reward and Bayesian feedback, and up to four return half-unit steps. Affordability requires strictly positive arrival energy. Maintenance death precedes belief transition. Transition surviving learners with hazard 0.05; transitioning a blank belief at birth or in the first period leaves it at 0.5. Record action and bookkeeping with no numeric tolerance for energy. Use the C07 1e-12 tie tolerance for observation site and patch. Death is permanent. With no new sensory outcome, only transition the prior. Reward units are 128 or 192 ticks.

Periods are indexed 0–239; founders have founding period −1, newborns act from the following period. Process active deaths in individual traversal order before end-period births. Randomise eligible parent order by full shuffle before applying slot limits. Replay each active death and birth in the same order, choosing uniformly from currently living individuals for both events. Each passive replay has independently generated mutation and demographic draws and the same initial founder genotype ordering. Retain individual event identities to verify genealogy and timing.

Use one executable process for numerical simulation, single-threaded post-processing and complete raw evidence. Separate named random streams for mapping, founder order, individual resource states, observation, reward, propensity, ties, mutation, and reproductive ordering. Across settings match seed mapping/founder schedules and draw opportunities keyed by period and serial individual ID, without treating descendants as matched individuals. Record evaluation seed identities and settings. Output compact binary or columnar files with a schema. Reconstruct energy, genotype and demographic counts independently from the raw records, and verify passive cohort and pedigree dynamics. Validate zero mutation, no reproduction and deterministic rewards on distinct fixtures and benchmark only seeds 3590/3591 before unlocking seed 3600 onwards.

Compute active and passive end-period count of L=1 and S>0 integrated over 240 periods with denominator 28,800. Retain zeros for any extinct periods. Subtract each world's eight-replay mean. Compare stable cheap reward 8 overhead 0 versus zero, stable cheap minus unpredictable cheap, and stable cheap minus stable expensive, pairing the same 20 world seeds. Report mean, sample SD, 95% two-sided t interval using t(19) and n. No multiplicity adjustment, directional filtering or imputation. Report all settings with genotype abundance, population composition and survival. Distinguish population-weighted from world-level frequency and restrict composition denominators to existing organisms, flagging empty denominators. Report mutation, lineage persistence, observed lifetime and births with starting-period exposure by genotype, including censoring at the horizon. Include behavioural and energy summaries and conditional passive counts. Exploratory figures and summaries will not be used to retrofit the contrasts.
